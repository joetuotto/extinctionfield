# Neurological Spectrum — DATA/Research Proposals

Unexecutable [DATA] items from Codelle: Epilepsia x Migreeni x Klusteripaaansarky — Q-tekija-spektri.

## D1: Chronic migraine prevalence vs. EMF technology adoption
- Correlate chronic migraine prevalence trends (1990-2025) with cumulative EMF technology adoption (mobile, WiFi, LED) across countries
- Use ITU technology adoption data and WHO/national migraine prevalence data
- Test: countries with earlier and denser EMF adoption show steeper migraine prevalence increase

## D2: ELF-priming CSD threshold experiment
- Expose cortical slices to chronic ELF (50 Hz, 7 days), then measure CSD threshold (KCl concentration needed to trigger CSD)
- Repeat with gabapentin co-treatment
- Prediction: ELF lowers CSD threshold; gabapentin co-treatment normalizes it

## D3: Cluster headache EMF exposure case-control
- Case-control study with detailed EMF exposure assessment (occupation, residential proximity, device use)
- Compare EMF exposure history in cluster headache patients vs. matched controls
- Prediction: cluster headache patients have statistically higher lifetime EMF exposure

## D4: SUDEP urban vs. rural registry study
- Registry study comparing SUDEP incidence in urban vs. rural epilepsy patients
- Control for seizure frequency and medication compliance
- Prediction: urban epilepsy patients have higher SUDEP rate after controlling for confounders

## D5: Psilocybin + EMF reduction RCT in cluster headache
- RCT of psilocybin + EMF reduction protocol vs. psilocybin alone in episodic cluster headache
- Prediction: combined intervention produces longer remission than psilocybin alone

## D6: Neonatal rodent EMF seizure model
- Expose neonatal rodents (P3-P7, before KCC2 switch) to pulsed GSM 900 MHz at mobile-phone intensity without picrotoxin
- Prediction: neonatal animals show seizure activity or epileptiform EEG changes without pharmacological pre-treatment, while adult animals do not
