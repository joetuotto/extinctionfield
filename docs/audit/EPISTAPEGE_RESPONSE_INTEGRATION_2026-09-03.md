# Epistapege and response-condition integration audit

Date: 2026-09-03  
Model owner: BERM  
Measurement branch: FieldState v2 (optional input only)

## Decision

Epistapege is a first-class qualitative branch of the BERM civilization model.
Its canonical website route is `/civilization/epistapege`. It is not a
FieldState concept, does not publish a numerical forecast and is not connected
to ASFR/TFR as a fitted coefficient.

The explicit route is:

```text
HPA/HPG + melatonin/redox + androgen-receptor-use state
  -> population distribution of behavioural weighting
  -> accessible narrative attribution
  -> omission of the upstream state from the explanatory data model
  -> institutional reuse of the downstream report
```

The evidence status differs by transition. Limited causal access, endocrine
binding, neural motivation and physiological-attitude associations are direct
component findings. Their union into the complete route is composed
convergence. The survey-to-institution transition and its population effect
remain open and require one joint longitudinal protocol.

## Response-condition integration

The attached literature narrows the *form* of the BERM response problem. A
universal weighted channel sum is not admissible. The response must allow:

- vector/background and polarization dependence;
- envelope spectrum and finite coherence duration;
- circadian, metabolic and developmental phase;
- receptor subtype, agonist state, redox state and genotype;
- organ transfer and exposure history; and
- positive, null or negative channel interaction.

The model now records the exact multichannel Lindgren expansion, including
`n(n-1)/2` pair terms, and stores additive and multiplicative interaction
contrasts without fixing their sign. These are algebraic and experimental-
design constraints, not a calibrated tissue response.

## Evidence classes

1. `DIRECT_SAME_PROTOCOL`: exposure, endpoint and targeted intervention occur
   in the same protocol.
2. `DIRECT_COMPONENT`: one transition or response condition is directly
   observed, but not the complete BERM route.
3. `COMPOSED_CONVERGENCE`: separately observed components are joined by a BERM
   inference with explicit assumptions.
4. `OPEN_L2`: the endpoint-specific geometry-to-tissue mapping is not yet
   calibrated.

No direct experiment was invented to close these gaps. Existing studies now
constrain how the experiments and kernels must be specified.
