Unexecutable [DATA] items from Codelle: KONVERGENSSIVERIFIOINTI — Ultimaattinen integraatio-ohje.

## D1: Chelation + EMF reduction superadditivity RCT
- RCT: chelation therapy + EMF reduction vs chelation alone vs EMF reduction alone in EHS patients with elevated heavy metal levels
- Measure: EHS symptom scores, heavy metal panel (blood Cd, Pb, MeHg), CaMKII Thr286 phosphorylation
- Prediction: combined group shows >50% improvement vs <25% for either alone (superadditive effect)

## D2: PGC × occupational EMF cross-sectional study
- Cross-sectional study correlating pineal gland calcification volume (CT/MRI) with occupational EMF exposure history
- Cohort: electricians, telecom workers (high EMF) vs office workers, farmers (low EMF), age-matched
- Prediction: high-EMF occupations have significantly higher PGC volume

## D3: Cadmium tissue accumulation × EMF dosimetry
- Occupational cohort with equal dietary/occupational Cd exposure but different EMF exposure
- Measure: tissue Cd levels (blood, kidney cortex biomarkers) + personal EMF dosimetry
- Prediction: high-EMF group has higher tissue Cd accumulation at equivalent external Cd levels (Cav3.1 window current mechanism)

## D4: MeHg neurotoxicity threshold × EMF comparison
- Compare neurodevelopmental outcomes in Faroe Islands (high MeHg, low EMF) vs urban Japan (high MeHg, high EMF) at equivalent MeHg exposure
- Use existing cohort data from both populations
- Prediction: urban Japan shows neurotoxicity effects at lower MeHg thresholds

## D5: ETH Zürich nimodipine-5G follow-up
- Extension of Sousouri 2025 protocol: administer nimodipine (L-type Ca²⁺ blocker) before 5G exposure
- Measure: sleep EEG changes across CACNA1C genotypes
- Prediction: nimodipine abolishes genotype-dependent sleep EEG changes
- Note: this is BERM's Level 1 falsification test — failure would collapse the model

## D6: Amish chronic disease trend comparison
- Compare age-adjusted incidence trends (1990-2025) for T2D, obesity, autism, ADHD, depression, myopia, SIDS
- Amish health registries vs CDC NHANES general US population
- Prediction: Amish show stable or declining rates where general population shows increase
- Note: this is BERM's Level 2 falsification test

## D7: EMF reduction intervention RCT
- RCT of comprehensive EMF reduction (shielded sleeping environment, wired devices, no LED at night) in symptomatic individuals, 3 months
- Biomarker panel: CaMKII Thr286 phosphorylation (lymphocytes), sleep quality (actigraphy), melatonin (urine 6-sulfatoxymelatonin), blood pressure
- Prediction: EMF reduction group shows significant improvement in all four biomarkers
- Note: this is BERM's critical missing interventional evidence

## D8: Walker sleep chain longitudinal measurement

## D9: Breast milk melatonin × baby monitor proximity
